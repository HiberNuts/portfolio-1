## beUI

The scenario selector in `src/components/workflow.tsx` adapts the shared-layout spring interaction from beUI Tabs. Its layout and styling are custom; the full beUI component library is not bundled.

Source: https://beui.dev/components/motion/tabs
Registry: https://beui.dev/r/tabs.json
